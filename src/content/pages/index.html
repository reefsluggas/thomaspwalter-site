Are you ready to party?

Working with me

The whole year, start to finish

Here's what the year before your wedding looks like when I'm your DJ. Seven steps, each with a short video of the real thing.

See how it works
  1. You check the dateIf I'm free you'll see it right away and can book a call on the spot. If I'm already booked, you get a few DJs I trust.
  2. We talk for twenty minutesPhone or video. After we talk I send a written quote, and it matches the website.
  3. You lock it inContract and deposit online. You get the planning app and one page with every link you'll need.
  4. You plan, at your paceTimeline, must plays, do not plays, ceremony songs. Tips arrive when you'll actually need them.
  5. We check in at three monthsA call with every couple. Where the plan is, what's stuck, and getting to know you.
  6. The final run-throughWe confirm the timeline, name pronunciations, special songs and any remaining details.
  7. Wedding dayI'm there hours before your guests. I run the plan we built together so you can live in the moment.
