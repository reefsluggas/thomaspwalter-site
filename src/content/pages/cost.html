What moves the price

  • Day and month. Saturdays in April through November are the most expensive dates for every vendor. Non-Saturday dates and off-season dates are discounted.
  • Ceremony sound. If your ceremony is somewhere other than the reception room, that's a second sound system, more mics and another hour of setup.
  • Lighting. Uplighting the room in your colors and moving lights for the dance floor are the two most common add-ons.
  • Hours. Some DJs charge by the hour and bill overtime. I don't. Unlimited hours from one setup is included, because I don't book anything else on your day.
  • Travel. Anything more than about 50 miles outside Pittsburgh usually adds a travel fee.
  • The venue. Some rooms need more gear than others. A second system for a ceremony on the lawn, extra speakers for a long room, a longer load-in. That's why I quote each wedding on its own.
  • Who shows up. With a one-person operation, the DJ you meet is the DJ who plays your wedding. With a company, ask.

What you should get for that money

A full-time professional with hundreds of weddings behind them. Backup equipment for everything. A planning system so your timeline and your music don't live in a text thread. Someone who will meet with you as many times as you need. Wireless mics that work. A DJ who mixes, so there's no dead air between songs. And an MC who makes announcements as needed without being overly talkative.

Questions people ask me about price

Is a DJ worth it?
Your guests won't remember the napkins. They'll remember whether they danced. The DJ runs the whole night from the first announcement to the last song.
When should we book?
Popular Saturdays go about a year out. If you have a date and a venue, you're ready.
Do you offer discounts?
Yes, for non-Saturday dates April through November, any off-season date, and active military and first responders.
What about the deposit?
About a third of the total to hold the date, with the balance due two to four weeks before the wedding. Cash, check, Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, CashApp or card.
