On the day

When I show up. Two to three hours before your guests, depending on the setup. I'll tell you the exact time at our final meeting so your venue or planner knows.

What I need. Not much. I bring my own booth. A spot near a power outlet, and if the ceremony or cocktail hour is somewhere else, I've got a battery-powered setup for that.

Food. A hot meal is appreciated, not required.

Requests on the night. If someone wants to hear something, they can just ask. If it's not on the list and not on my computer, I can usually download it on the spot. I'll work in guest requests if they fit the vibe.

Your other vendors. Give my number to anyone who needs to coordinate something with me: officiant, readers, photographer, videographer, venue coordinator, musicians, singers. I'd rather hear from them before the day than during it.
