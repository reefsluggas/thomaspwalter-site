Working with me

What it's like to work with me

Here's what the year before your wedding looks like when I'm your DJ, start to finish, so there are no surprises. If you'd rather watch than read, each step has a short video.

Video: the availability check on a phone, 30 seconds, your voice over it.
01

You check the date

You put in your date and venue. If I'm free, you see it right away and you can book a call on the spot. If I'm already booked, you get a list of a few DJs I trust.

Video: you at your desk explaining what you ask and why.
02

We talk for twenty minutes

Phone or video, your pick. I ask about the two of you, the venue, what you listen to in the car, and what you're nervous about. You ask me whatever you want. By the end we both know if I'm the right fit. After we talk I'll send a written quote, and it'll match what's on the Weddings page.

Video: the welcome email and the couples hub on a phone. Short.
03

You lock it in

A contract and a deposit, about a third of the total, secure your date. Everything's signed and paid online. Once it's done, you get your planning app and a page with every link you'll need from me. No digging through old emails.

Video: the planning app on your phone, you walking through the timeline and the lists.
04

You plan, at your pace

The planning app is where your timeline, your must plays, your do not plays and your ceremony songs live. It works on your phone. Invite your partner or your planner. Share the guest request link if you want your people adding songs. Along the way I send you the planning tips when you'll actually need them. Some couples fill it all in over a weekend. Some wait until the month before. Both are fine.

Video: you explaining what you look for on this call and the one question you always ask.
05

We check in at three months

A call I book with every couple. We look at where the plan is, I flag anything missing, and we talk through whatever's stuck. This is also when I get to know you, which is how I know what to play when nobody's told me.

Video: a real final meeting, with a couple who's up for it.
06

The final run-through

Two to four weeks out. We confirm the timeline, how to pronounce every name in the wedding party, the special songs and which versions, where I'm setting up, and who's giving toasts in what order. There may be a homework assignment or two.

Video: setup time-lapse into the first dance into the floor.
07

Wedding day

I'm there two to three hours before your guests. Your planner and photographer have my number. The ceremony audio is checked, cocktail hour is at a reasonable volume, and when it's time to dance I read the crowd all night. I execute the plan we created together so you can live in the moment.

After

You get a note from me, and if the night was what you hoped, I'll ask you to say so on Google.
