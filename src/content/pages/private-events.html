Private events

Will you DJ my non-wedding party? Yes.

That's been in my FAQ for years and it's still true. Birthdays, anniversaries, retirement parties, holiday parties, corporate events, bar and bat mitzvahs, reunions, fundraisers, afterparties. If people are supposed to have a good time, I'm interested.

Same approach as a wedding. I find out what you and your crowd actually listen to, I mix every song into the next so there's no dead air, I'm on the mic only when there's a reason to be, and I read the room all night. I bring the sound, the dance floor lights, the wireless mics and the backup gear.

What a private event usually looks like

  • A four to six hour party with one sound setup. More hours cost nothing extra.
  • Wireless mics for toasts, speeches or whoever wants to say something.
  • Dance floor lighting that moves with the music.
  • Room color lighting, club lights, a photo booth or a projected name or logo if you want them.
  • A planning call where you tell me the vibe and the no-go songs, then a shared list where you and your guests can add requests ahead of time.

Trivia and game nights too

I co-founded Trivia Jockeys, Pittsburgh's bar trivia company, so if your party wants a round of trivia, music bingo or a game show segment in the middle of the night, I host it myself with the same app we use at our bar trivia nights, and everyone plays from their phone. Couples have done it at rehearsal dinners and in the middle of the reception.

Pricing

Private events are priced by the day of the week, the hours and what you want to add. Tell me the date and what you have in mind and I'll put together a quote after we talk. For corporate events I can invoice the company directly.
