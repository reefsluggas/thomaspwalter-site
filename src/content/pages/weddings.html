Weddings

Remarkable weddings for remarkable people

Planning a once-in-a-lifetime event is no easy task, and my goal is to make my part of it as fun and stress-free as possible.

I help couples have the wedding they want by offering suggestions during planning, setting the right tone during the formalities, and packing the dance floor when it's time to cut loose. I'm not the diva DJ who has to be the center of attention. You're the stars of the show. The best part of this job is getting to know good people and figuring out their personalities so the vibe of the day is actually theirs.

We're called DJs, but there's a lot more to it than playing music. On wedding day I work as a team with the photographer, videographer, caterer, the venue and everyone else who has a hand in the day. There are no second chances, and the DJ can make or break the party. DJing isn't just my passion, it's my full-time job.

Pricing

I'm a DJ, not a salesperson.

It's important to me to be transparent about pricing without the nickel and diming. A lot of vendors are secretive and want to "discuss your vision" before they'll give you a number. I believe my fees are reasonable based on my skill level, professionalism and experience with weddings and events, so here they are.

Every quote is put together for your wedding, because no two venues or dates are the same. Saturdays April through November start at $2,500, and most couples spend around $2,950 for ceremony and reception coverage. Prices move with the time of year, the day of the week, how popular the date is, travel, and the equipment your venue needs. Discounts are available for non-Saturday dates April through November, any off-season date, and active military and first responders.

What's included, every time

  • Unlimited hours of reception coverage from one sound setup. No overtime charge if the party runs late.
  • A small sound setup for cocktail hour in a separate spot.
  • Me on the mic for all announcements, without being overly talkative.
  • Continuously mixed music with no dead air.
  • Wireless microphones for toasts and speeches.
  • Dance floor lighting that moves with the music.
  • Coordination with the rest of your vendor team.
  • Guest requests on the night, if you want them. I can usually download a song on the spot.
  • Custom music editing.
  • Backup equipment for everything.
  • Unlimited planning meetings with me. Phone, video or in person.
  • The planning app, with timeline, song picks, must plays, do not plays and a guest request link.

Planning

You get the planning app the day you book. Build your timeline, pick music for each moment, set your must plays and do not plays, and get to it from your phone or a computer. Invite your partner or your planner. Share the guest request link if you want to. I like to do a check-in call around three months out and a final run-through about a month before, and you can book as many meetings in between as you want.

Ceremony

For the "I dos," crystal clear audio through top-of-the-line microphones and speakers. Some people would call my ceremony setup overkill. It's that important to me for that important a moment. Prelude, processional and recessional music is yours to choose and played at the right volume at the right moment. Clip-on or handheld mics for the officiant and readers, and hookups for singers, musicians and your videographer.

Cocktail hour

You're off taking pictures while your guests have drinks and apps. The music is there to set the mood, not to be the focal point. People should be able to talk without yelling, and I'm careful about that. What you pick for this part sets the tone for the night, so choose wisely. I'm happy to make suggestions.

Reception

Let's party. Introductions, special dances, speeches, dancing and everything in between, I work with the rest of the vendor team so it all runs smooth. You relax and enjoy your party while I run the plan we built together. The dance party is my favorite part of the day. I read the crowd constantly to make sure everyone has a great time.
